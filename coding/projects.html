HeritageTrail project modules


These modules describe the portal planned in Experiments 1 to 4. This portfolio presents them as static project information.


1. Login and guest access


Provide login, registration and guest-access options.


2. Location selection


Choose Mysuru, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh or Tamil Nadu.


3. Heritage site discovery


Explore forts, museums, temples, monuments, cultural villages and historical buildings.


4. Site details and planning


Review history, visiting information and location before planning a visit.


5. Responsible tourism guidelines


Encourage visitors to avoid littering, respect local culture and protect monuments.


6. Visit planning and booking


Check visit details and show an availability response or booking reference.


7. Feedback and rating


Collect visitor feedback and a rating after the visit.
